## Part 2: Team Agreement

### 1. Team Values

**What values will guide your team?**
Aura and hype moments

### 2. Meetings

**How often will you meet?**
We will meet at least once a week, with extra meetings before major project deadlines when needed.

**When and where will you meet?**
We will usually meet during or after our Tuesday lab in person. If needed, we will meet online through Discord.

**What happens if someone cannot attend?**
They should tell the team as soon as possible, review any notes or decisions afterward, and complete any assigned follow-up work.

### 3. Communication

**What communication tools will you use?**
We will use Discord for day-to-day communication and GitHub for project work, issues, pull requests, and task tracking.

**Which tool will be used for urgent matters?**
Discord will be used for urgent matters, using an @ mention or direct message when a quick response is needed.

**How will important information be shared with the entire team?**
Important information will be posted in the main team Discord channel so everyone can see it, and project tasks or decisions will also be recorded on GitHub when relevant.

### 4. Response Expectations

**How quickly should team members respond to messages?**
Team members should normally respond within 24 hours. Urgent messages should be acknowledged as soon as reasonably possible.

**What should someone do if they cannot respond?**
They should let the team know as soon as possible and give an estimate of when they will be available to respond.

**What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?**
The team will check in with the member directly. If the problem continues, we will document it, discuss how to redistribute affected work, and involve the TA if necessary.

### 5. Respect & Professional Conduct

**What does respectful behavior mean to your team?**
We will communicate politely, listen to each other, give constructive feedback, respect everyone's time, and disagree without making things personal.

**What behaviors are unacceptable?**
Personal attacks, disrespectful comments, ignoring agreed responsibilities, taking credit for someone else's work, and repeatedly dismissing another member's ideas are unacceptable.

**How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?**
We will give everyone a chance to speak during meetings, ask for input before major decisions, and divide work fairly based on availability and workload.

### 6. Accountability

**What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?**
We will talk to the member directly, identify the problem, and agree on a plan to catch up. If the issue continues, work may be reassigned and the problem will be documented before involving the TA or instructor.

### 7. Decision-Making

**How will your team make decisions?**
We will vote on it, majority rules. In the event of a tie well flip a coin.

### 8. Conflict Resolution

**How will your team handle disagreements?**
We will address disagreements early and respectfully, focus on the issue rather than the person, and work toward a solution that is fair to the team.

**Describe the steps you will take to resolve conflicts within the team before involving the instructor or TA.**
First, the people involved will discuss the issue directly. If it is not resolved, the full team will discuss it and agree on a solution. We will document the plan and follow up. If the problem still cannot be resolved, we will involve the TA or instructor.

### 9. Anticipated Challenges

**Identify potential challenges your team may face and explain how you will address them.**
Scheduling conflicts, missed deadlines, unequal contributions, and poor communication. We will use clear task ownership, regular check-ins, early notice of problems, and shared planning to deal with these issues.
